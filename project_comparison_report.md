# General User Feature Gap Summary (Old vs New Awaken)

## Executive Summary for General Users

If an everyday user switches from the **Old Awaken App** to the **New Awaken App (`awaken_v1`)**, here is a plain-language summary of what features, visual fun, and conveniences they will **miss out on**, as well as what **new benefits** they will experience.

---

## 1. What the User Misses Out On (Gaps in New App)

### 🏃 1. Fewer Workout Choices to Turn Off the Alarm
- **Old App**: Offered **4 camera-verified exercises**: *Squats, Push-ups, Jumping Jacks, and High Knees*.
- **New App**: Only supports **2 exercises** (*Squats and Push-ups*). High Knees and Jumping Jacks are currently unavailable.

### 🎲 2. No "Tax Roulette" (Surprise Morning Workouts)
- **Old App**: Featured a "Tax Roulette" setting that picked a surprise exercise at random when your alarm fired, making sure you couldn't predict or get comfortable with just one routine.
- **New App**: You can only select a single fixed exercise ahead of time.

### 🗺️ 3. Less Crisp & Plain Map Styling
- **Old App**: Used smooth, high-definition **Vector Maps** with custom dark futuristic styling. Map text and lines remained razor-sharp at any zoom level.
- **New App**: Uses standard image-tile maps, which can look pixelated when zoomed in and consume more cellular data.

### 🌫️ 4. No "Fog of War" Map Unlocking
- **Old App**: Covered unexplored parts of the world map with a "Fog of War" layer, giving a satisfying video-game feel of uncovering hidden territory as you ran or walked.
- **New App**: Shows the standard open map everywhere, losing the mystery of exploring hidden zones.

### 🎯 5. Missing "Bounty Zones" on the Map
- **Old App**: Highlighted special real-world "Bounty Targets" on the map that rewarded you with extra points or bonuses if you ran through them to capture that zone.
- **New App**: Lacks bounty targets on the map.

### 👥 6. No Live Friend Status on the Ringing Alarm Screen
- **Old App**: While your alarm was actively ringing, a live "Squad Status Bar" showed on-screen so you could see which of your friends were waking up alongside you in real time.
- **New App**: Squad features are on a separate tab, but do not show live friend updates on the alarm ringing screen itself.

### 🔔 7. No Push Notifications for Territory Attacks or Expired Areas
- **Old App**: Sent instant phone notifications whenever a rival player invaded your captured territory ("Turf Hit Alert!") or when your claimed area was about to decay due to inactivity.
- **New App**: Does not send instant phone alerts when your territory is challenged or expiring.

### 🎨 8. No Custom Themes / Cyberpunk HUD Customization
- **Old App**: Had a theme picker where users could unlock and switch between custom neon, futuristic, and cyberpunk visual themes.
- **New App**: Only supports standard Light Mode and Dark Mode.

---

## 2. What the User Gains (Improvements in New App)

Even though some visual features are currently missing, the **New App** brings major real-world improvements for everyday users:

1. 🔒 **Alarms That Never Fail (100% Offline Reliability)**:
   - Your alarms, streak data, and workout records are stored safely in an offline-first database. Even if you lose internet or go into airplane mode, your alarm will ring and your progress will never be lost.
2. 🔋 **Guided Setup for Tricky Phones (Xiaomi, Samsung, Oppo)**:
   - Includes a built-in "Alarm Test & Reliability Page" and guided setup screens that prevent phone battery savers from killing your alarm in the background overnight.
3. ⚡ **Instant Guest Mode (No Immediate Sign-Up Required)**:
   - You can start using the app immediately without creating an account first, and link your Google or Email account later whenever you're ready.
4. 🏃‍♂️ **Uninterrupted GPS Tracking on Long Runs**:
   - Uses a background service so your distance and map path are accurately tracked even if your phone screen turns off or you switch to a music app during your run.
